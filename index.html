Research · NLP2024

Hausa → English neural machine translation

BLEU 5.88 → 11.85 after cleaning the corpus

Final-year project (grade A). Fine-tuned a MarianMT model (Helsinki-NLP opus-mt-ha-en) for a low-resource African language. Most of the gain came from the data: I cleaned a noisy 4,735-pair parallel corpus down to 3,284 reliable pairs, then trained for 10 epochs on an Apple M1.

  • PyTorch
  • Transformers
  • MarianMT
  • Low-resource NLP
